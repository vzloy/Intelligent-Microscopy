# ZEISS Lattice SIM 5 — Super-Resolution Structured Illumination Microscope

## System Reference for Imaging & Microscopy Workflows

**Version date:** 2026-08-28

This document describes a ZEISS Lattice SIM 5 super-resolution structured illumination microscopy system built on an Axio Observer 7 SR inverted microscope stand. The configuration includes a four-line laser module (405/488/561/640 nm), Lattice SIM illumination module with SIM² reconstruction, dual ORCA Fusion BT sCMOS cameras via DuoLink SR, Viluma 9 nine-channel LED for widefield fluorescence, AI Sample Finder condenser, Definite Focus 3 hardware autofocus, SuperZ piezo stage (500 µm), environmental control (heating + CO2), a comprehensive DIC outfit, and eight objective lenses ranging from 10× to 63×. Three workstations are included: an acquisition PC, a processing/storage PC (81 TB), and a third offline processing PC. It is intended as a machine-readable reference for designing acquisition workflows, analysis pipelines, and experiment planning with LLM-based tools.

## 2. Lattice SIM Illumination & Detection

### 2.1 Illumination Module

| Component | Code | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Illumination module Lattice SIM 5 | 000000-2592-313 | Core SIM module for SIM Apotome and extended Lattice SIM; includes cable set for the system |

The Lattice SIM 5 illumination module is attached to the rear port of the microscope stand and provides fully motorised SIM imaging. Key properties:

- Lattice spot pattern (not grid lines) provides higher contrast, faster imaging speed, and half the light exposure compared to conventional SIM grid patterns.
- Five different grating frequencies for Lattice SIM, optimally matched to laser wavelength and objective lens.
- One grating for SIM Apotome.
- Motorised exchange of gratings in multi-colour Lattice SIM.
- Fast piezo-actuated phase stepping of gratings.
- Supports two acquisition modes: **Lattice SIM** (super-resolution) and **SIM Apotome** (fast optical sectioning).
- Combined with **SIM²** reconstruction algorithm for doubled SIM resolution (down to ~60 nm lateral).
- Laser coupling via polarisation-maintaining single-mode fibre (no user adjustment of laser coupling required).

## 12. Acquisition Modes & Imaging Modalities

### 12.1 Lattice SIM — Super-Resolution

The primary modality. A lattice spot pattern is projected onto the sample through the objective. Multiple phase-shifted pattern images are acquired and computationally reconstructed. Because the lattice pattern is intrinsically two-dimensional, only translational repositioning is required (no rotation), dramatically increasing imaging speed compared to classical grid SIM.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Illumination pattern | Lattice spot pattern (2D; translational repositioning only, no rotation) |
| Reconstruction algorithm | SIM² (dual-iterative; doubles conventional SIM resolution) |
| Lateral resolution (SIM²) | ~60 nm (typical FWHM with 63×/1.40 Oil DIC, 100 nm beads, 488 nm excitation; sample- and SNR-dependent) |
| Lateral resolution (conventional SIM) | ~120 nm |
| Compatible objectives | Plan-Apochromat 63×/1.40 Oil DIC; C-Apochromat 63×/1.20 W Corr; alpha Plan-Apochromat 63×/1.46 Oil (ACR coded) |
| Multi-colour | Up to 4 fluorescent labels (sequential detection) + simultaneous dual-colour with DuoLink |

### 12.2 SIM Apotome — Fast Optical Sectioning

Uses structured illumination (1D line grid) for fast optical sectioning of larger volumes with crisp contrast and high resolution. Combined with SIM² for enhanced resolution.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Illumination pattern | One-dimensional line grid |
| Resolution (SIM/SIM² Apotome, 25×/0.8) | ~320/265 nm lateral (typical FWHM, 100 nm beads, 488 nm excitation) |
| Compatible objectives | Plan-Apochromat 40×/1.4 Oil; C-Apochromat 40×/1.2 W; LD LCI Plan-Apochromat 25×/0.8 Imm Corr DIC; Plan-Apochromat 20×/0.8 Air; EC Plan-Neofluar 10×/0.3 Air |
| Light dose | Lower than confocal — suitable for live-cell imaging |

## 16. System Limitations & Notes for Workflow Design

- **Not a confocal.** Lattice SIM is a widefield-based super-resolution technique using structured illumination. There is no pinhole, no confocal sectioning, and no laser-scanning. Optical sectioning is achieved computationally (SIM reconstruction) or via structured illumination (SIM Apotome). For laser-scanning confocal, point-scanning, or multiphoton imaging, use a confocal system.
- **No STED.** Super-resolution is limited to SIM² (~60 nm lateral). For STED nanoscopy (< 50 nm), use a STED-equipped system.
- **No multiphoton.** There is no tuneable IR pulsed laser. Two-photon and three-photon excitation are not available.
- **No FLIM.** There is no time-correlated single-photon counting or lifetime detection hardware. For fluorescence lifetime imaging, use a FLIM-equipped system.
- **No TIRF illumination module.** Although the 63×/1.46 TIRF objective is installed, the illumination module is configured for Lattice SIM, not TIRF. The objective's high NA benefits SIM resolution but the system cannot perform true TIRF illumination.
- **Four laser lines only.** Excitation is limited to 405, 488, 561, and 640 nm. There is no tuneable laser or white-light laser. Fluorophores must be chosen to match these lines.
- **SIM² requires high SNR raw images.** The SIM² algorithm doubles conventional SIM resolution but requires sufficient signal in the raw pattern images. Very dim samples may not reconstruct well at full SIM² resolution; fall back to conventional SIM or SIM Apotome.
- **Sample thickness limitation.** SIM works best on thin samples (single cells, monolayers, thin tissue sections). Deep tissue and thick organoids will show degraded SIM reconstruction quality. SIM Apotome is more tolerant of sample thickness but has lower resolution.
- **Cover glass sensitivity.** High-NA oil objectives (63×/1.40, 63×/1.46) require #1.5H high-performance cover glasses (0.17 mm). The multi-immersion 25× and 40× objectives have correction collars for variable cover-glass thickness (0–0.17 mm and 0.15–0.19 mm respectively). Temperature-specific immersion oils are provided for 23 °C, 30 °C, and 37 °C work.
- **DuoLink QUAD enables 4-colour but via 2-step sequential.** Four-colour Lattice SIM requires switching between the two DuoLink QUAD filter cubes; it is not fully simultaneous four-channel. True simultaneous acquisition is two channels.
